Create a new company dimension.

The page is visible with the `access.crm.create` grant.

## Pages routes

- `/crm/company_dimensions` — List, search and manage all company dimensions.
- `/crm/company_dimensions/create` — Create a new company dimension. *(this page)*

## What you can do here

### How to create a new company dimension
1. On the **Company Dimensions** list page, click **New** in the toolbar.
2. Fill in the fields below — required: **12**, **0**, **1**, **2**, **3**, **4**.
3. Click **Save** — the new company dimension is created.
