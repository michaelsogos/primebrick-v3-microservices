List, search and manage all contacts.

Each row shows the Type, the Value, the Label, plus the status flags Default.

The page is visible with the `access.crm.customer_contacts` grant.

## Pages routes

- `/crm/customer_contacts` — List, search and manage all contacts. *(this page)*
- `/crm/customer_contacts/create` — Create a new customer contact.

## What you can do here

### How to create a new contact
1. Click **New** in the toolbar.
2. Fill in the form — required: **15**, **0**, **1**, **2**, **3**, **4**.
3. Click **Save** — the new contact is created.

### How to delete a contact
1. Open the row actions menu on the contact and click **Delete**.
2. Confirm the deletion in the dialog — the contact is soft-deleted and can be restored.

### How to restore a deleted contact
1. Set the deleted-records filter to **Deleted only** or **All records** to show recoverable records.
2. Open the row actions menu on the contact and click **Restore**.
3. Confirm in the dialog — the contact is active again.

### How to view the change history of a contact
1. Open the row actions menu on the contact and click **Audit history**.
2. The version history of the contact will appear on the right sheet panel.

## Actions

### Row actions

| Action | Status | Permission | What it does | Require Step-Up Auth |
|--------|--------|------------|--------------|----------------------|
| **create** | ENABLED | `crm.customer_contact.create.single` | Creates a new contact | no |
| **update** | DISABLED | `crm.customer_contact.update.single` | Opens the contact for editing | no |
| **delete** | ENABLED | `crm.customer_contact.delete.single` | Marks the contact as deleted but keeps it so it can be restored | no |
| **restore** | ENABLED | `crm.customer_contact.restore.single` | Brings a soft-deleted contact back | no |
| **purge** | DISABLED | — | Removes the contact forever | no |
| **audit history** | ENABLED | `crm.customer_contact.read.audit` | Shows the change history of the contact | no |

- Creating a new contact is available on this page — the create action opens the create form in the same tab and it requires the `crm.customer_contact.create.single` permission.
- Editing a contact is not available on this page — if enabled, it would require the `crm.customer_contact.update.single` permission.
- Soft-deleting a contact is available on this page — marks the contact as deleted but keeps it so it can be restored and it requires the `crm.customer_contact.delete.single` permission.
- Restoring a soft-deleted contact is available on this page — brings a soft-deleted contact back and it requires the `crm.customer_contact.restore.single` permission.
- Permanently deleting a contact is not available on this page — no permission is declared for it on contacts.
- Viewing the change history of a contact is available on this page — every contact keeps a full version history and it requires the `crm.customer_contact.read.audit` permission.

### Bulk actions

| Action | Status | Permission | What it does | Require Step-Up Auth |
|--------|--------|------------|--------------|----------------------|
| **delete** | ENABLED | `crm.customer_contact.delete.bulk` | Marks the selected contacts as deleted | no |
| **restore** | ENABLED | `crm.customer_contact.restore.bulk` | Restores the selected contacts | no |
| **duplicate** | ENABLED | `crm.customer_contact.duplicate.bulk` | Duplicates the selected contacts | no |
| **export** | DISABLED | — | Exports the list | no |

- Soft-deleting the selected contacts is available on this page — marks the selected contacts as deleted and it requires the `crm.customer_contact.delete.bulk` permission.
- Restoring the selected contacts is available on this page — restores the selected contacts and it requires the `crm.customer_contact.restore.bulk` permission.
- Duplicating the selected contacts is available on this page — duplicates the selected contacts and it requires the `crm.customer_contact.duplicate.bulk` permission.
- Exporting the list is not available on this page — no permission is declared for it on contacts.
