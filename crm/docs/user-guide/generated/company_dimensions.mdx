List, search and manage all company dimensions.

Each row shows the Code, the Label translation, the Min size, the Max size, the Sort.

The page is visible with the `access.crm.company_dimensions` grant.

## Pages routes

- `/crm/company_dimensions` — List, search and manage all company dimensions. *(this page)*
- `/crm/company_dimensions/create` — Create a new company dimension.

## What you can do here

### How to create a new company dimension
1. Click **New** in the toolbar.
2. Fill in the form — required: **12**, **0**, **1**, **2**, **3**, **4**.
3. Click **Save** — the new company dimension is created.

### How to delete a company dimension
1. Open the row actions menu on the company dimension and click **Delete**.
2. Confirm the deletion in the dialog — the company dimension is soft-deleted and can be restored.

### How to restore a deleted company dimension
1. Set the deleted-records filter to **Deleted only** or **All records** to show recoverable records.
2. Open the row actions menu on the company dimension and click **Restore**.
3. Confirm in the dialog — the company dimension is active again.

### How to view the change history of a company dimension
1. Open the row actions menu on the company dimension and click **Audit history**.
2. The version history of the company dimension will appear on the right sheet panel.

## Actions

### Row actions

| Action | Status | Permission | What it does | Require Step-Up Auth |
|--------|--------|------------|--------------|----------------------|
| **create** | ENABLED | `crm.company_dimension.create.single` | Creates a new company dimension | no |
| **update** | DISABLED | `crm.company_dimension.update.single` | Opens the company dimension for editing | no |
| **delete** | ENABLED | `crm.company_dimension.delete.single` | Marks the company dimension as deleted but keeps it so it can be restored | no |
| **restore** | ENABLED | `crm.company_dimension.restore.single` | Brings a soft-deleted company dimension back | no |
| **purge** | DISABLED | — | Removes the company dimension forever | no |
| **audit history** | ENABLED | `crm.company_dimension.read.audit` | Shows the change history of the company dimension | no |

- Creating a new company dimension is available on this page — the create action opens the create form in the same tab and it requires the `crm.company_dimension.create.single` permission.
- Editing a company dimension is not available on this page — if enabled, it would require the `crm.company_dimension.update.single` permission.
- Soft-deleting a company dimension is available on this page — marks the company dimension as deleted but keeps it so it can be restored and it requires the `crm.company_dimension.delete.single` permission.
- Restoring a soft-deleted company dimension is available on this page — brings a soft-deleted company dimension back and it requires the `crm.company_dimension.restore.single` permission.
- Permanently deleting a company dimension is not available on this page — no permission is declared for it on company dimensions.
- Viewing the change history of a company dimension is available on this page — every company dimension keeps a full version history and it requires the `crm.company_dimension.read.audit` permission.

### Bulk actions

| Action | Status | Permission | What it does | Require Step-Up Auth |
|--------|--------|------------|--------------|----------------------|
| **delete** | ENABLED | `crm.company_dimension.delete.bulk` | Marks the selected company dimensions as deleted | no |
| **restore** | ENABLED | `crm.company_dimension.restore.bulk` | Restores the selected company dimensions | no |
| **duplicate** | DISABLED | — | Duplicates the selected company dimensions | no |
| **export** | DISABLED | — | Exports the list | no |

- Soft-deleting the selected company dimensions is available on this page — marks the selected company dimensions as deleted and it requires the `crm.company_dimension.delete.bulk` permission.
- Restoring the selected company dimensions is available on this page — restores the selected company dimensions and it requires the `crm.company_dimension.restore.bulk` permission.
- Duplicating the selected company dimensions is not available on this page — no permission is declared for it on company dimensions.
- Exporting the list is not available on this page — no permission is declared for it on company dimensions.
