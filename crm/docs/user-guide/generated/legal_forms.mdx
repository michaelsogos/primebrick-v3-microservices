List, search and manage all legal forms.

Each row shows the Code, the Country, the ELF code, the Local name, the Abbreviation, the Status.

The page is visible with the `access.crm.legal_forms` grant.

## Pages routes

- `/crm/legal_forms` — List, search and manage all legal forms. *(this page)*
- `/crm/legal_forms/create` — Create a new legal form.

## What you can do here

### How to create a new legal form
1. Click **New** in the toolbar.
2. Fill in the form — required: **15**, **0**, **1**, **2**, **3**, **4**, **5**, **6**, **7**.
3. Click **Save** — the new legal form is created.

### How to delete a legal form
1. Open the row actions menu on the legal form and click **Delete**.
2. Confirm the deletion in the dialog — the legal form is soft-deleted and can be restored.

### How to restore a deleted legal form
1. Set the deleted-records filter to **Deleted only** or **All records** to show recoverable records.
2. Open the row actions menu on the legal form and click **Restore**.
3. Confirm in the dialog — the legal form is active again.

### How to view the change history of a legal form
1. Open the row actions menu on the legal form and click **Audit history**.
2. The version history of the legal form will appear on the right sheet panel.

## Actions

### Row actions

| Action | Status | Permission | What it does | Require Step-Up Auth |
|--------|--------|------------|--------------|----------------------|
| **create** | ENABLED | `crm.legal_form.create.single` | Creates a new legal form | no |
| **update** | DISABLED | `crm.legal_form.update.single` | Opens the legal form for editing | no |
| **delete** | ENABLED | `crm.legal_form.delete.single` | Marks the legal form as deleted but keeps it so it can be restored | no |
| **restore** | ENABLED | `crm.legal_form.restore.single` | Brings a soft-deleted legal form back | no |
| **purge** | DISABLED | — | Removes the legal form forever | no |
| **audit history** | ENABLED | `crm.legal_form.read.audit` | Shows the change history of the legal form | no |

- Creating a new legal form is available on this page — the create action opens the create form in the same tab and it requires the `crm.legal_form.create.single` permission.
- Editing a legal form is not available on this page — if enabled, it would require the `crm.legal_form.update.single` permission.
- Soft-deleting a legal form is available on this page — marks the legal form as deleted but keeps it so it can be restored and it requires the `crm.legal_form.delete.single` permission.
- Restoring a soft-deleted legal form is available on this page — brings a soft-deleted legal form back and it requires the `crm.legal_form.restore.single` permission.
- Permanently deleting a legal form is not available on this page — no permission is declared for it on legal forms.
- Viewing the change history of a legal form is available on this page — every legal form keeps a full version history and it requires the `crm.legal_form.read.audit` permission.
