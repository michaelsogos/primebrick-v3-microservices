List, search and manage all customer accounts.

Each row shows the Type, the Company name, the Legal form, the Country, plus the status flags Default.

The page is visible with the `access.crm.customer_accounts` grant.

## Pages routes

- `/crm/customer_accounts` — List, search and manage all customer accounts. *(this page)*
- `/crm/customer_accounts/create` — Create a new customer account.

## What you can do here

### How to create a new customer account
1. Click **New** in the toolbar.
2. Fill in the form — required: **18**, **0**, **1**, **2**, **3**, **4**, **5**, **6**, **7**.
3. Click **Save** — the new customer account is created.

### How to delete a customer account
1. Open the row actions menu on the customer account and click **Delete**.
2. Confirm the deletion in the dialog — the customer account is soft-deleted and can be restored.

### How to restore a deleted customer account
1. Set the deleted-records filter to **Deleted only** or **All records** to show recoverable records.
2. Open the row actions menu on the customer account and click **Restore**.
3. Confirm in the dialog — the customer account is active again.

### How to view the change history of a customer account
1. Open the row actions menu on the customer account and click **Audit history**.
2. The version history of the customer account will appear on the right sheet panel.

## Actions

### Row actions

| Action | Status | Permission | What it does | Require Step-Up Auth |
|--------|--------|------------|--------------|----------------------|
| **create** | ENABLED | `crm.customer_account.create.single` | Creates a new customer account | no |
| **update** | DISABLED | `crm.customer_account.update.single` | Opens the customer account for editing | no |
| **delete** | ENABLED | `crm.customer_account.delete.single` | Marks the customer account as deleted but keeps it so it can be restored | no |
| **restore** | ENABLED | `crm.customer_account.restore.single` | Brings a soft-deleted customer account back | no |
| **purge** | DISABLED | — | Removes the customer account forever | no |
| **audit history** | ENABLED | `crm.customer_account.read.audit` | Shows the change history of the customer account | no |

- Creating a new customer account is available on this page — the create action opens the create form in the same tab and it requires the `crm.customer_account.create.single` permission.
- Editing a customer account is not available on this page — if enabled, it would require the `crm.customer_account.update.single` permission.
- Soft-deleting a customer account is available on this page — marks the customer account as deleted but keeps it so it can be restored and it requires the `crm.customer_account.delete.single` permission.
- Restoring a soft-deleted customer account is available on this page — brings a soft-deleted customer account back and it requires the `crm.customer_account.restore.single` permission.
- Permanently deleting a customer account is not available on this page — no permission is declared for it on customer accounts.
- Viewing the change history of a customer account is available on this page — every customer account keeps a full version history and it requires the `crm.customer_account.read.audit` permission.

### Bulk actions

| Action | Status | Permission | What it does | Require Step-Up Auth |
|--------|--------|------------|--------------|----------------------|
| **delete** | ENABLED | `crm.customer_account.delete.bulk` | Marks the selected customer accounts as deleted | no |
| **restore** | ENABLED | `crm.customer_account.restore.bulk` | Restores the selected customer accounts | no |
| **duplicate** | ENABLED | `crm.customer_account.duplicate.bulk` | Duplicates the selected customer accounts | no |
| **export** | DISABLED | — | Exports the list | no |

- Soft-deleting the selected customer accounts is available on this page — marks the selected customer accounts as deleted and it requires the `crm.customer_account.delete.bulk` permission.
- Restoring the selected customer accounts is available on this page — restores the selected customer accounts and it requires the `crm.customer_account.restore.bulk` permission.
- Duplicating the selected customer accounts is available on this page — duplicates the selected customer accounts and it requires the `crm.customer_account.duplicate.bulk` permission.
- Exporting the list is not available on this page — no permission is declared for it on customer accounts.
