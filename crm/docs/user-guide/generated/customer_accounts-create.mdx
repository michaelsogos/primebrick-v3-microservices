Create a new customer account.

The page is visible with the `access.crm.create` grant.

## Pages routes

- `/crm/customer_accounts` — List, search and manage all customer accounts.
- `/crm/customer_accounts/create` — Create a new customer account. *(this page)*

## What you can do here

### How to create a new customer account
1. On the **Customer Accounts** list page, click **New** in the toolbar.
2. Fill in the fields below — required: **18**, **0**, **1**, **2**, **3**, **4**, **5**, **6**, **7**.
3. Click **Save** — the new customer account is created.
