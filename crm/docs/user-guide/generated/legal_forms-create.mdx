Create a new legal form.

The page is visible with the `access.crm.create` grant.

## Pages routes

- `/crm/legal_forms` — List, search and manage all legal forms.
- `/crm/legal_forms/create` — Create a new legal form. *(this page)*

## What you can do here

### How to create a new legal form
1. On the **Legal Forms** list page, click **New** in the toolbar.
2. Fill in the fields below — required: **15**, **0**, **1**, **2**, **3**, **4**, **5**, **6**, **7**.
3. Click **Save** — the new legal form is created.
