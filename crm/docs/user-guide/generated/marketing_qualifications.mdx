List, search and manage all marketing qualifications.

Each row shows the Code, the Label translation, the Sort.

The page is visible with the `access.crm.marketing_qualifications` grant.

## Pages routes

- `/crm/marketing_qualifications` — List, search and manage all marketing qualifications. *(this page)*
- `/crm/marketing_qualifications/create` — Create a new marketing qualification.

## What you can do here

### How to create a new marketing qualification
1. Click **New** in the toolbar.
2. Fill in the form — required: **10**, **0**, **1**, **2**.
3. Click **Save** — the new marketing qualification is created.

### How to delete a marketing qualification
1. Open the row actions menu on the marketing qualification and click **Delete**.
2. Confirm the deletion in the dialog — the marketing qualification is soft-deleted and can be restored.

### How to restore a deleted marketing qualification
1. Set the deleted-records filter to **Deleted only** or **All records** to show recoverable records.
2. Open the row actions menu on the marketing qualification and click **Restore**.
3. Confirm in the dialog — the marketing qualification is active again.

### How to view the change history of a marketing qualification
1. Open the row actions menu on the marketing qualification and click **Audit history**.
2. The version history of the marketing qualification will appear on the right sheet panel.

## Actions

### Row actions

| Action | Status | Permission | What it does | Require Step-Up Auth |
|--------|--------|------------|--------------|----------------------|
| **create** | ENABLED | `crm.marketing_qualification.create.single` | Creates a new marketing qualification | no |
| **update** | DISABLED | `crm.marketing_qualification.update.single` | Opens the marketing qualification for editing | no |
| **delete** | ENABLED | `crm.marketing_qualification.delete.single` | Marks the marketing qualification as deleted but keeps it so it can be restored | no |
| **restore** | ENABLED | `crm.marketing_qualification.restore.single` | Brings a soft-deleted marketing qualification back | no |
| **purge** | DISABLED | — | Removes the marketing qualification forever | no |
| **audit history** | ENABLED | `crm.marketing_qualification.read.audit` | Shows the change history of the marketing qualification | no |

- Creating a new marketing qualification is available on this page — the create action opens the create form in the same tab and it requires the `crm.marketing_qualification.create.single` permission.
- Editing a marketing qualification is not available on this page — if enabled, it would require the `crm.marketing_qualification.update.single` permission.
- Soft-deleting a marketing qualification is available on this page — marks the marketing qualification as deleted but keeps it so it can be restored and it requires the `crm.marketing_qualification.delete.single` permission.
- Restoring a soft-deleted marketing qualification is available on this page — brings a soft-deleted marketing qualification back and it requires the `crm.marketing_qualification.restore.single` permission.
- Permanently deleting a marketing qualification is not available on this page — no permission is declared for it on marketing qualifications.
- Viewing the change history of a marketing qualification is available on this page — every marketing qualification keeps a full version history and it requires the `crm.marketing_qualification.read.audit` permission.
