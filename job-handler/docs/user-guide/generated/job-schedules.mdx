List, search and manage all job schedules.

Each row shows the Job, the Service, the Kind, the Schedule, the Type, the Status, the Run Stats, the Armed at, the Updated at.

The page is visible with the `access.system.job.job-schedules` grant.

## What you can do here

### How to delete a job schedule
1. Open the row actions menu on the job schedule and click **Delete**.
2. Confirm the deletion in the dialog — the job schedule is soft-deleted and can be restored.

### How to restore a deleted job schedule
1. Set the deleted-records filter to **Deleted only** or **All records** to show recoverable records.
2. Open the row actions menu on the job schedule and click **Restore**.
3. Confirm in the dialog — the job schedule is active again.

### How to view the change history of a job schedule
1. Open the row actions menu on the job schedule and click **Audit history**.
2. The version history of the job schedule will appear on the right sheet panel.

## Actions

### Row actions

| Action | Status | Permission | What it does | Require Step-Up Auth |
|--------|--------|------------|--------------|----------------------|
| **create** | DISABLED | `_authenticated_admin` | Creates a new job schedule | no |
| **update** | DISABLED | `_authenticated_admin` | Opens the job schedule for editing | no |
| **delete** | ENABLED | `_authenticated_admin` | Marks the job schedule as deleted but keeps it so it can be restored | no |
| **restore** | ENABLED | `_authenticated_admin` | Brings a soft-deleted job schedule back | no |
| **purge** | DISABLED | — | Removes the job schedule forever | no |
| **audit history** | ENABLED | `_authenticated_admin` | Shows the change history of the job schedule | no |

- Creating a new job schedule is not available on this page — if enabled, it would require the `_authenticated_admin` permission.
- Editing a job schedule is not available on this page — if enabled, it would require the `_authenticated_admin` permission.
- Soft-deleting a job schedule is available on this page — marks the job schedule as deleted but keeps it so it can be restored and it requires the `_authenticated_admin` permission.
- Restoring a soft-deleted job schedule is available on this page — brings a soft-deleted job schedule back and it requires the `_authenticated_admin` permission.
- Permanently deleting a job schedule is not available on this page — no permission is declared for it on job schedules.
- Viewing the change history of a job schedule is available on this page — every job schedule keeps a full version history and it requires the `_authenticated_admin` permission.
