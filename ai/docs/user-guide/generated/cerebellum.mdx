The **Cerebellum** panel opens in a side sheet. It is delivered by the owning module as a remote component mounted inside the host sheet surface.

## Inputs

| Input | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| `models` | passed by the caller when the panel opens |
| `assistants` | passed by the caller when the panel opens |
| `rows` | passed by the caller when the panel opens |
| `onCreated` | passed by the caller when the panel opens |

## What it reads

- `PUT /api/v1/entities/ai_cerebellum/{param}`
- `POST /api/v1/entities/ai_cerebellum`
